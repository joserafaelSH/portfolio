# About me

I'm a backend-focused software engineer. I spend most of my time in Go and TypeScript, building services that have to stay correct under concurrency, partial failure, and load — task queues, event pipelines, and the boring-but-critical plumbing that keeps the rest of a product working.

This site is itself one of those projects: a terminal emulator that doubles as a portfolio. Type `help` to see what it can do, or `ls` to poke around.

## What I care about

- Systems that fail predictably and recover automatically.
- Observability — if it can break, it should tell you before a user does.
- Simple, boring solutions over clever ones, until clever is actually necessary.
